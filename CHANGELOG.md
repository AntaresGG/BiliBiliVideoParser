## [1.0.0] - 2026-08-15

### Added

- BiliLens source-card, timeline, evidence-chain, reception-field, and insight workflow.
- Resolution for Bilibili URLs, short links, BV/av IDs, bangumi ep/ss IDs, and multi-part videos.
- Anonymous metadata, subtitle, and optional danmaku extraction.
- Human-subtitle preference with AI-subtitle and local faster-whisper fallbacks.
- Markdown, TXT, JSON, SRT, and standalone HTML rendering.
- Installable Codex Plugin and standalone Skill layouts.
- English, Simplified Chinese, and Japanese documentation.
- Offline tests, CI checks, release automation, and community health files.
