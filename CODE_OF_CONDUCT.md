# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and considerate.
- Critique ideas and code rather than people.
- Respect privacy, copyright, and platform access boundaries.
- Accept correction and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Keep technical discussions relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing, credential sharing, deliberate copyright abuse, and sustained disruption are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact address in the plugin manifest. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in public spaces.
